The Spirit of Cricket

Under the sun, the field is bright,
The players gather, ready to fight.
A bat in hand, a ball in flight,
They chase their dreams from left to right.

The bowler runs with steady pace,
The batsman stands with focused face.
A mighty shot, the crowd cheers loud,
The game brings joy to every crowd.

A wicket falls, the cheers grow strong,
Yet both teams know they must play on.
With every run and every catch,
They give their best in every match.

From dusty grounds to stadium light,
Cricket fills our hearts with delight.
Win or lose, the spirit stays—
A game of passion, dreams, and praise.
